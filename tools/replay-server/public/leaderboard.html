How points work

A mission win earns 100 points; second earns 97; third earns 95. Fourth starts at 94, then points scale down using the number of ranked players across the selected source.

Only each player’s fastest eligible time counts per mission. Tied times share place and points (1, 1, 3). Equal total points share overall rank. Group filters change which missions are summed, not the points formula.
